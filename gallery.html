Institutional Archives • Care Home 2018 • 2 Photographs

Care Home 2018 — Rebuild Kerala Flood Relief Initiative

Initiative: Construction and key handover of newly constructed houses under the supervision and initiative of the Society as part of Rebuild Kerala 2018 (flood relief initiative of the Kerala Government to reconstruct structures with the help of co-operative societies across Kerala).
First House Handover: Key handover officially done by the Hon. Joint Registrar (General) of Co-operative Societies, Wayanad in the presence of Hon. President Sri. Sunny Joseph.
Second House Handover: Key handover officially done by Hon. MLA Kalpetta Assembly Constituency Sri. C. K. Saseendran in the presence of Hon. President Sri. Sunny Joseph, Managing Committee members, and dignitaries.
