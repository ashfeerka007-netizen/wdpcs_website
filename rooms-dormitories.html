Accommodation Guidelines for Police Personnel

  • All occupants must produce valid Kerala Police Department identification / official duty order at check-in.
  • Booking confirmations and receipt vouchers generated by the external booking application must be presented upon arrival.
  • Discipline and decorum appropriate to the Kerala Police fraternity must be strictly maintained at all times.
  • For special group duty allotments or urgent administrative assistance, please contact the Society Office helpdesk directly.
