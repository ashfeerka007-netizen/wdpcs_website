Instructions for Document Submission

  1. Download and print the relevant prescribed application form on standard A4 paper.
  2. Complete all fields in legible handwriting, providing your official Police Department PEN / General Number and contact details.
  3. Attach required supporting documentation (e.g., salary slip copy, department ID card copy, guarantor signatures if applicable).
  4. Submit the signed physical application directly at the Society office counter during official working hours.
