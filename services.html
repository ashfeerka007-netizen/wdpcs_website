Category A

Deposits & Savings Accounts

Secure thrift accounts formulated to provide healthy returns, capital safety, and financial liquidity for police personnel and their families.

Deposit Account 1

Savings Deposit

Intended For: All active and eligible member police personnel needing regular liquid savings.

A convenient and flexible savings account facilitating daily financial transactions and systematic member thrift with safety and steady yield.

  • Easy deposit and withdrawal facilities at society counter.
  • Passbook facility and periodic statement generation.
Minimum Balance ₹500.00 (for accounts with withdrawal facility)
Interest Calculation Daily Balance Basis • Credited Bi-annually (30 September & 31 March)
Interest Rate As determined periodically by the Registrar of Co-op Societies
Withdrawal Limits Up to 4 withdrawals per week (relaxation by Secretary upon request)
Passbook Facility Free initial passbook • Duplicate issued on nominal fee
Account Opening Prescribed form, 2 passport photos & specimen signature in person
Nomination & Security Full nomination facility • Inoperative safeguards (2-year dormant rule)
Maintenance Charge Nominal ₹10.00 per year ledger maintenance fee

Deposit Account 2

Recurring Deposit

Intended For: Members wishing to build a substantial lump-sum through fixed monthly contributions.

A disciplined monthly savings plan where members deposit a predetermined amount each month over an agreed duration to earn attractive cumulative interest.

  • Ideal for future planned expenditures like children's schooling or home needs.
  • Automatic salary recovery integration where authorised.
Minimum Monthly Deposit ₹100.00 or in multiples of ₹100 per month
Tenure Options 12 Months to 120 Months (in multiples of 12 months)
Interest Rate As determined periodically by the Registrar of Co-op Societies
Deposit Loan Facility Up to 75% of paid-up deposit balance (at RD Rate + up to 2%)
Premature Closure Permitted under special circumstances (interest payable at SB rate)
Eligibility & Accounts All Society members • Single, Multiple, or Joint accounts with Nomination
Passbook Facility Official passbook issued • Duplicate issued on nominal fee
Download Application Form Enquire
Deposit Account 3

Fixed Deposit

Intended For: Members seeking secure term investment with assured interest returns.

Term deposit offering guaranteed security and competitive returns for member funds deposited for a specific duration.

  • Loan against Fixed Deposit facility available for emergent needs.
  • Flexible tenure options with periodic interest payout or cumulative maturity.
Minimum Amount ₹500.00 (Min. ₹25,000 for monthly/quarterly interest payout)
Minimum Tenure 15 Days (up to multi-year flexible tenures)
Interest Payout Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annually, or at Maturity
Auto Transfer Direct credit to depositor's SB Account or Loan Account
Deposit Loan Facility 80% to 90% of deposit value (at FD Interest Rate + 1% min)
Eligibility & Accounts Individuals, Joint accounts, Institutions & Minors (with Guardian option)
Nomination & Security Nomination available • Certificate signed by 2 authorised signatories
Premature Closure Permitted as per Society rules (applicable rate adjustment)

Category B

Monthly & Group Schemes

Specialized structured monthly thrift and collective advance schemes providing flexible salary-based instalments, credit advances, and mutual benefits.

Thrift Scheme 1

Monthly Savings Scheme

Intended For: Member police personnel wanting tailored monthly thrift options.

Specialized savings scheme engineered to encourage regular monthly thrift with favorable bonus interest provisions upon timely completion.

  • Convenient monthly instalment structures designed around police salaries.
  • Safe and reliable institutional backing with up to 90% advance facility.
Group Size & Tenure 20 to 100 Months / Members
Ticket Limit Max 10% of tickets or 5 tickets per individual (whichever is lower)
Advance Facility Up to 90% of deposited balance (secured by salary/sureties)
Completion Payout Disbursed in 30 days after deduction of 5% Society Commission
Passbook Facility Official passbook issued • Duplicate issued on nominal fee
Eligibility A-Class and D-Class members of the Society
Portfolio Cap Up to 40 Groups & ₹4 Crores total scheme portfolio
Transferability Permitted with Society's consent (if no advance is availed)

Group Scheme 2

Group Deposit Scheme

Intended For: Member groups and unit personnel seeking mutual co-operative savings.

A collective co-operative deposit mechanism facilitating mutual thrift and monthly credit access among member police personnel.

  • Monthly lottery draw or bidding advance (70% to 95% of monthly pool).
  • Dividend sharing: Excess pool balance distributed as discount on next month.
Group Size & Tenure 25 to 100 Members / Months (multiples of ₹100)
Monthly Advance 70% to 95% of group instalment pool
Draw Schedule 2nd Saturday of every month at 11:00 AM
Dividend Sharing Excess sum after 5% service charge shared equally as discount
Passbook & Duplicate Official passbook issued • Duplicate issued on nominal fee
Eligibility A-Class and D-Class members & Society employees
Enquire Details →
