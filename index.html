Institutional Background

About the Society

The Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 is an established credit co-operative society constituted to foster mutual financial assistance and thrift among employees of the Kerala Police Department who are working or residing in Wayanad District, Kerala.

Operated under democratic co-operative values, the society provides reliable financial services including member loans, structured deposit schemes, academic welfare awards, and accommodation services.

About Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd No. W 208

Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd No. W 208 is a credit co-operative society serving eligible employees of the Kerala Police Department who work or reside in Wayanad District, Kerala. The society was registered on 24 May 1996 and started operations on 21 August 1996. The society currently operates as a Class 1 Special Grade Society. Its services include deposit schemes, savings schemes, member loans, application facilities, statements, certifications and other member-oriented financial services.

Thrift & Group Schemes

Monthly & Group Schemes

Structured monthly thrift and collective advance mechanisms designed around police salary cycles.

  • Monthly Savings Scheme: 20–100 month structured instalments with up to 90% advance facility and completion bonus.
  • Group Deposit Scheme: Monthly mutual pool mechanism with advance borrowing and regular dividend distributions.

Member Credit Facilities

Member Loans

Low-friction credit facilities designed to support police personnel with emergency expenses, assets, and festivals.

  • Emergency Loan: Rapid credit up to ₹1,00,000 at 10% (12 Months, Non-EMI).
  • Hire Purchase Loan: Household durables & assets up to ₹2,00,000 at 10% (Up to 48 Months, Non-EMI).
  • Medium Term Loan: Higher credit limit up to ₹20,00,000 at 10% (Up to 120 Months, EMI).
  • Festival & Deposit Loans: Seasonal advances up to ₹20,000 at 9% (5 Months) & deposit-backed credit.
