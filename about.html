About the Society

Empowering our police force through financial solidarity, member-centric co-operation, and dedicated welfare service in Wayanad District.

Financial Solidarity

Empowering Our Police Force Through Financial Solidarity

Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 was registered on 24 May 1996 and commenced its operations on 21 August 1996.

The society was established with a vital purpose: to provide police personnel with a dependable and dedicated financial support system at a time when access to institutional credit was challenging.

The society was established under the visionary leadership of the district police leadership along with dedicated leaders of the district's police associations. From its inception, the society has remained committed to safeguarding the financial well-being of its members and supporting the welfare and progress of the Wayanad police community.

Dedicated Standards

Our Commitment

The society is steadfastly committed to upholding institutional integrity and member welfare through clear principles:

  • Serving Financial Needs: Promptly addressing the credit and financial needs of our members.
  • Financial Security: Promoting financial security and responsible financial practices across the force.
  • Accessible Facilities: Providing accessible savings products and timely credit facilities.
  • Family Welfare: Supporting the welfare, education, and social security of members and their families.
  • Transparent Administration: Maintaining transparent, audited, and responsible administration.
  • Mutual Co-operation: Strengthening the spirit of co-operation and mutual support.
  • Shared Growth: Continuing to grow alongside our members and adapting to evolving requirements.
