General Information

The Wayanad District Police Co-operative Society Ltd. No. W 208 is a registered primary credit co-operative society established under the Kerala Co-operative Societies Act to provide financial assistance, thrift promotion, and welfare support exclusively for employees of the Kerala Police Department.

Services are intended for employees of the Kerala Police Department who are currently stationed, working, or permanently residing within Wayanad District, Kerala, and have been admitted as members according to society bye-laws.

Deposits & Savings

The Society offers pure deposit accounts (Savings Deposit, Recurring Deposit, and Fixed Deposit) as well as structured monthly thrift schemes (Monthly Savings Scheme and Group Deposit Scheme).

Members can download the prescribed deposit application form from our Downloads section, complete the required particulars with nominee details, and submit it at the Society counter.

The Society offers Savings Bank accounts with the following key facilities:

  • Minimum Balance: ₹500.00 (for accounts with withdrawal facility).
  • Interest Calculation: Calculated on Daily Minimum Balance and credited bi-annually on the last working day of September and March.
  • Withdrawal Frequency: Up to 4 withdrawals per week (relaxations permitted by the Secretary upon request).
  • Passbook Facility: Initial passbook issued free of cost; must be presented for counter transactions.
  • Nomination & Security: Full nomination facility available. Accounts without customer transactions for 2 consecutive years are placed under secure dormant custody.
  • Annual Maintenance: Nominal fee of ₹10.00 per year for account maintenance.

Recurring Deposits provide systematic monthly savings with the following terms:

  • Minimum Monthly Deposit: ₹100.00 or in multiples of ₹100 per month.
  • Tenure Options: 12 Months to 120 Months (in multiples of 12 months).
  • Loan Against RD: A-Class members can avail a loan of up to 75% of their deposited balance (at RD rate + up to 2%).
  • Premature Closure: Permitted under special circumstances upon written request; interest is credited at Savings Bank (SB) rate.
  • Account Types: Single, multiple, or joint accounts with nomination facility and passbook recording.

Fixed Deposits offer secure investment with the following features:

  • Minimum Deposit Amount: ₹500.00 (Minimum ₹25,000.00 required for monthly or quarterly interest payouts).
  • Minimum Tenure: 15 Days (up to multi-year term options).
  • Interest Payout: Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annually, or at Maturity.
  • Auto Credit: Interest can be transferred directly to your Savings Bank (SB) or Loan Account.
  • Loan Against FD: Members can borrow 80% to 90% of deposit value (at nominal interest rate).
  • Account Options: Available for individual, joint, institutional, and minor accounts with nomination facility.

The Monthly Savings Scheme (MSS) is designed for structured monthly thrift:

  • Group Size & Duration: Formed with 20 to 100 members over 20 to 100 monthly instalments.
  • Individual Holding Limit: A member can subscribe up to a maximum of 10% of total group tickets or 5 tickets (whichever is lower).
  • Advance Facility: Non-defaulting subscribers can avail an advance of up to 90% of paid-up balance secured by salary certificate, FD, or sureties.
  • Maturity Payout: Disbursed within 30 days of completion after deduction of 5% Society Commission.
  • Eligibility: Open to all eligible members of the Society.

The Group Deposit Scheme (GDS) enables collective thrift and monthly credit access:

  • Group Structure: Formed in groups of 25 to 100 members / monthly instalments (fixed in multiples of ₹100).
  • Monthly Advance: In each instalment, an advance of 70% to 95% of the group pool can be availed by one eligible member.
  • Draw Schedule: Draw is conducted on the 2nd Saturday of every month at 11:00 AM.
  • Dividend Sharing: Excess pool collection after service charge is distributed equally as an immediate instalment discount for the next month.
  • Eligibility: Open to A-Class and D-Class members and Society staff.

All deposit application forms (Savings Bank, Recurring Deposit, Fixed Deposit, MSS, and GDS) are available in PDF format on our Downloads page and directly at the Society office counter.

Member Loans

The Society provides 5 primary credit products: (1) Emergency Loan, (2) Hire Purchase Loan, (3) Medium Term Loan, (4) Festival Loan, and (5) Deposit Loan.

Members must submit the official prescribed application form along with a copy of their latest salary pay slip, police department ID card, and quotation/estimate (for hire purchase durables). Application forms can be downloaded from the Downloads section.

A Deposit Loan allows members holding active Fixed Deposits or Recurring Deposits with the Society to avail instant liquidity against their pledged deposit balance without having to prematurely close the account.

The Society provides official loan schemes with the following limits and terms:

  • Emergency Loan: Maximum ₹1,00,000.00 at 10% interest rate for 12 months (Non-EMI).
  • Hire Purchase Loan: Maximum ₹2,00,000.00 at 10% interest rate for up to 48 months (Non-EMI).
  • Medium Term Loan: Maximum ₹20,00,000.00 at 10% interest rate for up to 120 months (EMI).
  • Festival Loan: Maximum ₹20,000.00 at 9% interest rate for 5 months (Non-EMI).
  • Deposit Loan: Up to 90% of pledged Fixed/Recurring Deposit balance.

Full specifications and downloadable application forms are available on the Services page and Downloads section.
